abstract: Addressing the challenge of identifying tomato pests and diseases, this research utilized transfer learning with Google's Inception-V3 model to create an image classifier. The system achieved an 88.9% accuracy in recognizing key issues like cutworms, whiteflies, early blight, powdery mildew, and hornworms. Beyond identification, the tool provides immediate information on control methods, serving as a vital resource to minimize yield loss and support growers in managing crop health.
